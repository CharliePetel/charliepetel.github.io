Tina Schwarz

The career path of artist Tina Schwarz, born in 1977, has not been straightforward. Dance in Arnhem, fine arts and film in Maastricht, visual communication in Düsseldorf. So it's not surprising that her work always deals with the body in motion. In particular, she explores how emotions influence the way a person moves and changes in a given space, and how that environment (including other people) or space reacts to it. Tina Schwarz's work is characterized by a playful approach to movement and a fascination with the human body. She uses various media to explore the relationship between body and space, and how emotions influence the way we move and perceive our surroundings.
